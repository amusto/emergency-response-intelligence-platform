# Emergency Response Intelligence Platform (ERIP)

## Vision
ERIP is a multi-agency operational intelligence platform that combines incidents, resources, facilities, routing, travel-time analysis, and real-time situational awareness into a common operating picture.

## Goals
- Support command center decision making
- Support first responder navigation and assignments
- Demonstrate geospatial and operational intelligence concepts
- Showcase travel-time based decision support
- Provide a production-shaped architecture suitable for portfolio and interview discussions

## Target Audience
- Emergency Operations Centers
- Public Safety Agencies
- Emergency Management Teams
- Operational Intelligence Platforms
- Peregrine-style operational data platforms
