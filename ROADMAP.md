# Emergency Response Intelligence Platform Roadmap

## Vision
A multi-agency operational intelligence system combining incidents, responders, facilities, routing, travel-time analysis, and operational awareness.

## Phases

0. Foundation
1. Operational Map MVP
2. Operational Search
3. PostGIS Geospatial Search
4. Travel-Time Isochrones
5. Resource Allocation
6. First Responder PWA
7. Route Intelligence
8. Real-Time Operations
9. Coverage Analysis
10. Traffic Simulation
11. AI Copilot
12. Data Integration
13. Production Readiness

## MVP Target
Complete phases 0-4 to produce a shareable Peregrine-aligned demonstration.
